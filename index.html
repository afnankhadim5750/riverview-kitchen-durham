Our story

A riverside table in old Durham

Riverview Kitchen sits on Silver Street, in the old town, with the river just beyond the window. There is no website, no booking app, no fuss — just a room that fills up, a kitchen that cooks from scratch, and a view that does a lot of the talking.

People come for breakfast and stay for a second coffee. They come for the pancake stacks and the full English, and they come back because the staff remember them. Around 2,100 reviews on ThreeBestRated say the same thing in different words: generous plates, honest food, fair prices, and a friendly room.

It is the kind of café a city keeps close — the one you tell visitors about, and the one you hope still has a table when you get there.

The signature plate

The full English,
done properly.

Quality and quantity — that is what the reviews keep saying. A full English here is not a small plate with a big price. It is the works, cooked to order, with toast, and room at the table to enjoy it slowly.

Come hungry. Order the fry-up. Watch the river while the kitchen does its work.

How a visit works

No booking app. No website.
Just walk in.

Riverview Kitchen runs on Facebook and on footfall. Here is the whole process:

01

Come to Silver Street

20 Silver Street, Durham old town — down by the river. Look for the café, not a sign-up form.

02

Take a table

Sit in by the window or take a seat outside when the weather allows. Dogs are welcome too.

03

Order at the counter

Pancake stack, full English or egg Benedict — the kitchen cooks it to order while you settle in.

04

Stay a while

Eat slowly, order another coffee, watch the river. Takeout is there if you are in a hurry.

Dogs welcome

Bring the dog.
They already know.

Riverview Kitchen is a dog-friendly café in the truest sense: dogs are part of the room, not an exception to it. Regulars walk in with their dogs, and the dogs settle under the tables like they own the place.

One reviewer’s dog even got a sausage. That is the level of welcome we are talking about.
